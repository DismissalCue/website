Our Mission

Safer dismissals for every school

Driveline streamlines school dismissal with secure family check-in, real-time parent notifications, and comprehensive staff handoff records. Built for safety, designed for simplicity.

Features

Built for schools, parents, and safety

Family Check-In

Separate adult accounts with per-child pickup claims ensure only authorized guardians can check out students.

Real-Time Notifications

Parents receive instant SMS and email notifications when their child is checked in or out.

Staff Handoff Records

Complete audit trail of who picked up each child, when, and which staff member authorized the release.

Permission Scoping

Role-based access control ensures staff, parents, and administrators only see what they're authorized to access.

Near-School Detection

GPS-based proximity detection allows parents to check in only when they're actually near the school.

Open Source

Built in the open with full transparency. Self-host or contribute to the project on GitHub.

How It Works

Simple for everyone

1

Parent Arrives

Guardian arrives at school and opens the Driveline app on their phone.

2

Check-In Request

Parent submits a pickup claim for their child. GPS confirms they're near the school.

3

Staff Verification

School staff see the request, verify the guardian's identity, and release the child.

4

Handoff Logged

All parties receive confirmation. The handoff is recorded with timestamp and staff signature.
