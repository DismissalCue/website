Features

Built for safety, speed, and peace of mind

GPS Geofencing

Define custom pickup zones around your school. Parents can only submit pickup requests when physically present within the geofence, eliminating fraudulent check-ins and ensuring safety.

Instant Staff Alerts

The moment a parent enters the geofence, staff receive a real-time notification with student name, grade, photo, and authorized pickup person — prepare students before parents even park.

Authorized Pickup Only

Per-child authorization lists ensure only pre-approved guardians can request pickup. Multi-adult family accounts with individual permissions prevent unauthorized releases.

Complete Audit Trail

Every pickup is logged with timestamp, GPS coordinates, guardian identity, staff member authorization, and digital signature — full compliance documentation for legal and safety requirements.

Reduces Wait Times by 60%

Geofencing allows staff to prepare students before parents arrive at the pickup point. No more long car lines or confused dismissals — students walk directly to waiting vehicles.

Bank-Level Security

Multi-factor authentication (MFA), role-based access control, encrypted data transmission, and FERPA-compliant data handling protect sensitive student and family information.

How It Works

Simple for everyone

1

Parent Enters Geofence

Guardian drives into the school's designated pickup zone. GPS automatically detects their arrival — no manual check-in needed.

2

Staff Notified Instantly

Teachers receive an alert with student name, photo, and authorized pickup person. Student is called and prepared for pickup before parent even parks.

3

Safe Handoff

Staff verify guardian identity and release the child. Entire process takes 30 seconds instead of 5 minutes — no long waits, no confusion.

4

Parents Notified

All authorized family members receive instant app notification with pickup time and staff member name. Complete audit trail stored for compliance.
