Features

Built for safety, speed, and peace of mind

GPS Geofencing

Set the school’s pickup area. Phone check-in verifies arrival within that area. Families who cannot use phone check-in can ask the office for its supervised pickup procedure.

Classroom Notifications

After an approved check-in, staff call the assigned classroom. Teachers acknowledge the call, and staff record the student’s movement and readiness.

Authorized Pickup Only

Each adult has a separate account and school-approved permissions for each child. Staff check the approved receiving adult before recording handoff.

Complete Audit Trail

Arrival, classroom and handoff steps have recorded times and staff attribution. Raw location evidence is kept separate from retained custody facts.

Keep the Line Moving

Give office, classroom and curbside staff a shared view of dismissal progress, from an approved arrival to a recorded handoff.

Access Controls

Multi-factor authentication, school-scoped roles and per-child permissions control access. Hosted service connections use HTTPS.

How It Works

Simple for everyone

1

Parent Checks In

While safely stopped at school, the approved adult opens the app, selects eligible children and checks in. Optional reminders never check in automatically.

2

Staff Call the Classroom

Staff call the assigned classroom. The teacher acknowledges the call and follows the school’s supervision and staging procedure.

3

Staff Record Handoff

Staff match the student and receiving adult to the approved pickup request, then record the physical handoff.

4

Pickup Status Updates

Adults with the school-approved information rights can follow pickup status and view the recorded handoff. Pickup permission and history access are separate.
