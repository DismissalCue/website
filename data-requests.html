Privacy requests

Account deletion and data requests

Proposed policy — review draft. Prepared October 25, 2026. This is not yet the effective policy. The proposed retention periods and deletion commitments require owner approval and implementation verification before publication.

You can request deletion of your DismissalCue adult account and eligible associated data, or request access or correction, without reinstalling the app.

Send a request

Email privacy@dismissalcue.com with the subject “DismissalCue account deletion request”. For access or correction, use that request type in the subject instead.

Include the email address used for your DismissalCue account, the school name, and what you are requesting. Please use your account email address if you can. If you no longer control that address, say so; do not send identity documents by email.

Never send passwords, passkeys, authenticator or recovery codes, a child’s precise location, or a full student roster.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge the request and use an appropriate trusted channel to verify it. Sending an email alone does not authorize access to someone else’s records.
  2. We identify the account data eligible for removal and any school-controlled records or documented preservation requirements.
  3. We arrange any necessary safe handover of an active pickup or staff control role, end account access, and process eligible removal.
  4. We confirm completion and explain any retained information and its reason or applicable schedule. The proposed completion target for eligible account removal is 30 days after verification.

A request is not a completed deletion. Removing the app, signing out or leaving one school does not, by itself, delete the adult account.

School records and backups

Deleting an adult account does not automatically delete a child’s school roster, another adult’s account, or the school’s custody and handoff history. Those records follow the district’s documented schedule and applicable preservation requirements. Ask the school about a student-record request; contact us if you need help directing it.

Eligible deleted data ages out of rolling backups on the proposed 35-day schedule. Deletion records must be reapplied before a restored service resumes. We do not promise immediate physical erasure from immutable backups.

Read the proposed privacy and retention policy for the full explanation.

Need help with pickup today?

Contact the school office for its supervised pickup procedure. A deletion or recovery request does not approve pickup, clear a safety hold or authorize a release.

For DismissalCue service help, contact support@dismissalcue.com.
