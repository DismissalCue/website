That's why we're building DismissalCue. To help families and schools stay connected, from the moment a parent arrives to the moment a child heads home.

Picture checking in for your children together. The school sees who's here, checks pickup permissions, and calls the right children. Staff still confirm the handoff, in person.

And pickup doesn't have to look the same for every child. Soccer on Tuesdays? Art club on Thursdays? The school sets each child's pickup time. Or, before a holiday weekend, it can schedule an earlier dismissal for the whole school.

Parents get clear updates about those plans. Staff have a shared picture of what's happening. And when someone needs help, they know which school office to contact.

It's about less confusion, and better support for the people looking after our children.
