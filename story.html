Meet DismissalCue. We're building a calmer, more connected way to get every school day home.

Imagine one check-in for eligible siblings, approved pickup permissions, and clear calls to staff. Your school stays in charge of the physical handoff.

School-set schedules. Local Ramadan timing. After-school clubs on the right days, for the right child. A later pickup shouldn't come as a surprise.

Clear updates for families. A shared view for staff. And school-specific help when an account or a pickup needs attention.

Less guesswork. Better coordination. More support for the people who care for our children.
