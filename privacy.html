Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Dismissalcue LLC operates DismissalCue for adult parents, guardians and school staff. This policy explains how we handle account information, school pickup records and location information in our apps, school workspace and website.

1. Your account and your school’s records

Your school arranges access to DismissalCue and approves each adult’s permissions for each child. An invitation or successful sign-in does not, by itself, authorize pickup. Children do not have child sign-in accounts.

Schools provide and control student rosters, classroom assignments, pickup permissions and dismissal records. DismissalCue processes those records to provide the school’s service under its agreement. Contact your school for changes to a student record or pickup arrangement. Contact us for help with your DismissalCue account or a privacy request.

2. Information we handle

  • Adult account information: name, email address, contact details provided for the service, school memberships, assigned duties, invitations and sign-in or recovery records.
  • School information: student names and school identifiers, classes and rooms, school-approved adult–child relationships, permission and restriction records, calendars and school help contacts.
  • Pickup information: requested children, arrival and cancellation times, classroom calls, supervision and readiness steps, the receiving adult, staff-recorded handoff and reconciliation records.
  • Location evidence: location and accuracy information processed when you choose phone check-in, together with the time and result of the proximity check. The check-in diagnostics store coarse bands rather than raw coordinates.
  • Device and security information: device or session identifiers, platform, IP address, sign-in events, registered public keys, error references and service logs needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Communications: school notices, delivery and acknowledgement records, and information you choose to include in support requests. If a school enables the optional staff assistant, its staff prompts and assistant records are also processed.

We do not receive Face ID or Touch ID templates. Your device performs biometric verification. Protected private signing keys stay on the device; the service may receive a public key, signed proof and verification result.

3. Location and optional reminders

When you choose to check in, the app requests foreground location to verify arrival near your school’s pickup area. A location match does not authorize a release: school staff still verify the receiving adult and record physical handoff.

Near-school reminders are optional. The reminder preference starts on, and you can turn it off. After a separate explanation and your operating-system permission choice, the app can use background geofences to offer a local reminder at about half a mile from school around pickup time. A reminder does not check you in automatically, grant pickup rights or record a handoff.

Reminder geofences are not a continuous location feed to the school. Staff do not receive a live map of your movements. Raw check-in coordinates are processed for your check-in; retained custody facts do not include those coordinates.

You can turn reminders off in the app and change location or notification permissions in device settings. If you cannot share location or use phone check-in, ask the office for its supervised pickup procedure.

4. Why we use information

  • Arrange school access, verify sign-in and enforce current school duties and per-child permissions.
  • Coordinate arrivals, classroom calls, supervised movement, physical handoff and reconciliation.
  • Provide school calendars, help information and notices to eligible recipients.
  • Support protected recovery, investigate problems, prevent misuse and maintain custody and security records.
  • Operate, diagnose and improve the service without placing advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers in the native apps.

We do not sell personal information or use school or student records for targeted advertising. The optional staff assistant cannot authorize custody or waive school permissions. Parent pickup does not depend on using the assistant.

5. Who can receive information

  • Your school: authorized personnel receive information needed for their assigned duties. Schools manage their staff access.
  • Approved adults: access depends on the school’s rights for that child. Pickup permission alone does not grant access to pickup history or parent alerts.
  • Service providers: hosting, delivery and support providers process information needed for their service. A configured model provider may process staff-assistant requests when the school enables that feature. We do not describe a provider as a recipient merely because we use its self-hosted software.
  • Required disclosures: we may disclose information when required by applicable law or valid legal process, or as necessary to address a security or safety incident. We assess the request and applicable school obligations.

The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Our public pages use system fonts and do not request external font resources. GitHub Pages may process technical connection information to serve the website.

Before a school uses live records, its agreement and provider information must identify the applicable processing arrangements and any material service-provider changes.

6. Retention periods

The periods below are operating limits, not claims that one law requires the same period for every school. A school must select its record-specific retention schedule before live student records are activated. A documented preservation requirement can delay eligible deletion; retained information remains restricted.

Deleting an account is different from deleting the school’s student or custody records. Existing encrypted backups expire within the rolling window; completed deletion records must be reapplied before restored service resumes. Minimal deletion evidence is retained separately to prevent erased accounts from returning after a restore. We do not silently rewrite a completed handoff.

7. Access, correction and deletion requests

You can request access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your adult account and eligible associated data. A separate school review may be needed for a student record or school-controlled information. We verify requests through an appropriate trusted channel before disclosing or deleting protected information.

Account deletion ends the adult account’s access. It does not approve a recovery, clear a pickup hold, remove another adult’s rights or create a new release. If an active pickup or a school control role needs a safe handover, we arrange that review and explain the result.

In the app, open Settings → Privacy → Delete my account, or use the account deletion and data-request page, or email privacy@dismissalcue.com. You can use the web request path after uninstalling the app. We confirm completion and explain any information retained and the reason for retaining it.

Do not email passwords, authenticator or recovery codes, identity-document photographs, or a child’s precise location. Do not include a full student roster in a support request.

8. Protecting information

Hosted connections use HTTPS. Access checks are scoped to accounts, schools, duties and child permissions. Adult school-data access requires multi-factor authentication; sensitive staff operations require recent passkey verification. Device storage protections help protect saved sign-ins and offline records.

No service can promise absolute security. Automated testing, independent assessment, operational recovery exercises and incident response are separate parts of release readiness. This policy does not claim an uncompleted audit, certification or compliance approval.

9. Your choices and children’s information

You can manage app reminders, device permissions and sign-in methods. Optional reminder settings do not change school pickup rights. Service or safety messages may still be necessary for the service.

DismissalCue is intended for adult users. Schools provide children’s school records; children do not register or use child sign-in accounts. Parents and guardians should ask the school about access to or correction of school records.

The clearly labelled simulated demo uses sample data. Its local actions do not check in or release a child at a school.

10. Changes and contact

We update this page and its effective date when the policy changes. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service or school contact channel before they take effect where required.
